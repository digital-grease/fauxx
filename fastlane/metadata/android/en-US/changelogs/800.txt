• New: every synthetic persona now browses in its own cookie jar. Cookies, site storage and cache picked up under one persona are invisible to the next, and are deleted when that persona retires, so a data broker cannot stitch two personas together through shared tracker cookies. This needs a recent Android System WebView; where that support is missing, personas share one jar as before and everything else still works (#242).
• Changed: personas now live one to three months instead of about nine days, at an interval that varies each cycle. A profile only becomes convincing once it has accumulated real browsing history, and resetting one every nine days on a predictable schedule was itself a pattern worth hiding. Channels phase the new persona in gradually, over a window that now scales with the persona's lifetime.
• Changed: Rotate Now explains what it costs. It switches every channel at once, whereas automatic rotations phase in gradually to avoid an obvious change-point.
• Removed: the custom User-Agent setting. Since v0.6.0 it only took effect with the Device Identity module switched off, and even then it could not match the persona's screen and navigator values. Device identity now comes from the active persona (#201).
• Fixed: several claims in the README, the store listing and the in-app copy that described client hints the app does not actually send, and described the browsing pool as process-isolated when it runs in a single process. The wording now matches what the app does.
• Maintenance: dependency updates.
